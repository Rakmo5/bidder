D2

Low-Level Design (LLD): End-to-End Data Processing Pipeline

Slide 5B Ready
01
Tender Ingestion & Rule Decomposition PDF → Layout Text → JSON

PyMuPDF chunks text page-by-page. LLM parses General Conditions (GCC) and Special Conditions (SCC), strictly applying SCC > GCC hierarchy into Pydantic TenderRequirement models.

02
Bidder Dossier Extraction & Forensic Fingerprinting Metadata & Trailer Parsing

Extracts audited turnover, years experience, ISO validity, and plant equipment. Concurrently extracts low-level PDF dictionary metadata: /Author, /Producer, creation timestamp, and document SHA256 hashes.

03
Anti-Cartelization & Collusion Forensic Audit Competition Act Sec 3

Cross-examines all competing bidders for identical author machines, timestamp proximity (< 30 mins), and validates the 18-digit Chartered Accountant UDIN format. Discovers duplicate UDINs and flags immediate Cartel Collusion Alerts.

04
Hybrid RAG Grounding & Compliance Scoring Clause-by-Clause Verification

Matches every tender requirement against bidder evidence. Verifies thresholds (e.g. ≥ ₹15 Cr turnover, ≥ 5 yrs exp, active ISO 45001). Extracts direct quotes with exact page numbers and generates a Technical Score (Ts: 0-100).

05
QCBS Scoring & Abnormally Low Tender (ALT) Risk Analysis GFR Rule 192 (70:30)

Computes normalized financial score Fs = (Fmin/F) * 100 and composite score S = (Ts * 0.70) + (Fs * 0.30). If a quote is > 20% below estimate, triggers ALT Warning and calculates a 5-year Life-Cycle Failure Risk Penalty.
